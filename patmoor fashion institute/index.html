About Patmoor Fashions

Fashion rooted in creativity, quality and purpose.

Patmoor Fashions is a fashion business and training academy dedicated to producing beautifully crafted garments and nurturing future fashion professionals through practical learning and guided experience.

Our mission is to help clients look their best through custom design and tailored production while giving aspiring creatives the practical skill set needed to thrive in the fashion industry.

Our mission

To deliver quality workmanship and creative excellence.

Our vision

To be a trusted destination for fashion and skill development.

Meet our CEO

Ms. Patience Aggor

Chief Executive Officer & Fashion Professional

Ms. Patience Aggor is a highly professional fashion leader with over seven years of hands-on experience in the fashion and dressmaking industry. Her dedication to excellence, creativity and quality workmanship continues to shape Patmoor Fashions into a trusted fashion house.

Beyond her outstanding leadership, Ms. Aggor is passionate about training and mentoring aspiring fashion professionals. Through practical, thoughtful and encouraging instruction, she has helped multitudes of students develop the skills and confidence needed to become accomplished professionals in their own right.

Her commitment to professionalism, personal growth and the preservation of quality craftsmanship remains at the heart of everything she teaches and every service she provides.
